All set up in 3 simple steps.

01

Download & install

Install GmailClass in your browser, then open the extension to get started.

02

Create a TypeSafe API key

Create your API key at TypeSafe and paste it into GmailClass. Your key stays in your browser.

03

Enable & reload Gmail

Choose your categories, enable the extension, and reload your Gmail page. Labels appear beside the emails in your inbox.

Meet Jev.
Your inbox’s instinct.

You describe what matters. Jev helps GmailClass spot it in your inbox, with labels that match your categories.

Discover TypeSafe ↗
  • Your words, your categories. Describe the mail you want to recognize. Jev checks each email against those descriptions.
  • A little more certainty. Each match comes with a probability. You set the threshold for when a label appears.
  • Room for more than one label. A travel receipt can be both a trip and an expense. Each category is checked on its own.
